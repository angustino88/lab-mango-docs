# Frequently asked questions

### I can sign in but I see "Access denied (403)".
The page needs a role you do not have. Spaces, Equipment, Finance and Insights are for coordinators and admins.
If you think you should have access, ask your lab admin to check your group ([Roles and access](./lab-admin/roles-and-access.md)).

### Why does my booking say "Pending"?
Bookings longer than **4 hours** need a coordinator's approval. Bookings of 4 hours or less are approved
straight away. See [Booking a bench](./lab-user/booking-a-bench.md#bookings-longer-than-4-hours).

### I made my approved booking longer and it is "Pending" again.
Extending a booking past 4 hours asks for a fresh decision, so the approval goes back to pending. Shortening
it to 4 hours or less keeps its current approval.

### Can I approve my own booking as a coordinator?
No. Nobody can approve or reject a booking they created or that is for them, admins included. Another
coordinator or admin decides.

### The bench I want is not free.
Two active bookings for one bench can never overlap. Pick another time or another bench.

### I got a role, but Lab Mango still shows the old menu.
Role changes apply on your next request. Reload the page. If the menu is still wrong, sign out and in again.

### Where do I report a broken instrument or bench?
Create a [support ticket](./lab-user/support-tickets.md) and link the resource, so its history shows the incident.
