# Getting started

## Sign in

1. Open Lab Mango in your browser.
2. Select **Log In** and sign in with your company Microsoft account (single sign-on). There is no separate
   Lab Mango password. If sign-in fails, select **Try Again**.
3. After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard**.

Your access comes from your company group membership. When you join a lab group you get the matching Lab Mango
role. If you can sign in but cannot create bookings, or a page says **Access denied (403)**, your account has no
role yet, or a lower one than the page needs. Ask your lab admin; see [Roles and access](./lab-admin/roles-and-access.md).

## Find your way around

The left menu groups the pages. Everyone sees:

| Menu | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Live overview of the lab: bench activity, bookings and resource status |
| **3D Dashboard** | The lab rooms in 3D; pick a room to see its stations and benches |
| **Leaderboard** | Bench usage rankings and weekly usage heatmaps |
| **Booking Calendar** | Book a bench and see bookings by day, week or month |
| **My Bookings** | The bookings you created: change, cancel, follow approval |
| **Support tickets** | Ask the lab team for help and follow your requests |

Coordinators and admins also see **Spaces**, **Equipment**, **Finance** and **Insights**. See the
[coordinator guide](./lab-coordinator/index.md).

In the header:

- the **book icon** opens this documentation;
- the **sun/moon icon** switches between light and dark mode.

## How statuses are shown

Lab Mango uses the same colours for statuses everywhere, so you can read a page at a glance:

| Colour | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Ready to use | Available, Operational, Approved |
| Blue | Busy but healthy | In use, In progress, Ordered |
| Cyan | Committed or access-limited | Reserved, Borrowed, Restricted |
| Amber | Needs attention soon | Maintenance, Pending, Calibration overdue |
| Red | Not usable, needs action | Broken, Out of service, Lost, Overdue |
| Grey | Out of circulation or unknown | Retired, Cancelled, Draft |

## Get help

- Something in the lab is broken or you need equipment: create a [support ticket](./lab-user/support-tickets.md).
- A question about how Lab Mango works: check the [FAQ](./faq.md), then ask your lab coordinator.
