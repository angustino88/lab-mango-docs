# Booking a bench

## Create a booking

1. Open **Booking Calendar** in the left menu.
2. Under **Benches**, choose the benches to show. **Quick picks** offers **My benches** (benches you have booked
   before) and **All**.
3. Either drag across a free time range in the calendar, or select **New Booking**.
4. Fill in the form:

   | Field | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | **Bench** | The bench you need |
   | **From Datetime** / **To Datetime** | Start and end of your session |
   | **Project** | The project the work is for |
   | **Booking reason** | A short description. **Your recent reasons** lets you reuse an earlier one |

5. Select **Create Booking**.

A booking cannot overlap another active booking on the same bench. If the time is taken, Lab Mango refuses the
booking. Choose another time or another bench.

## Bookings longer than 4 hours

- **4 hours or less:** approved straight away.
- **More than 4 hours:** Lab Mango asks **Approval Required**. Confirm to send the request to a coordinator.
  The booking is **Reserved** with approval **Pending** until a coordinator approves or rejects it.

If you later extend an approved booking beyond 4 hours, its approval goes back to **Pending**. Shortening a booking
keeps its current approval.

## Calendar views

Switch between **Month**, **Week**, day and list views at the top of the calendar; **Today** jumps back to now. The
calendar also shows bench downtime, such as maintenance windows, so you can plan around it.

You see other people's bookings to know when a bench is busy, but only your own bookings show their booking code
and can be changed by you.

## Booking statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reserved** | Booked and waiting for the start time |
| **In progress** | The session is running |
| **Completed** | The session finished |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you or a coordinator |
| **Expired** | The booked time passed without the session being used |

Approval is a separate field: **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**.

## Notifications

Where your lab has booking notifications turned on, Lab Mango notifies the person who created the booking, by
e-mail or Microsoft Teams, when the booking is created or when its bench, times, status, approval or project changes.
Changing only the reason sends nothing.
