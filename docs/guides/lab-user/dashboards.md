# Dashboards and leaderboard

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the live overview of the lab: which benches are free or busy, upcoming bookings, and the status
of resources. Bench activity uses a fixed colour language, for example green for **Free**.

## 3D Dashboard

The **3D Dashboard** shows the lab rooms in 3D. Select a room to look at its stations and benches and see what is
in use.

## Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** shows how the lab is used:

- bench usage rankings;
- usage personas such as *Power User*, *Steady Tester* and *Occasional Explorer*, based on sessions and bookings;
- weekly heatmaps (7 days × 24 hours) that show when benches are busiest. Use them to find quiet times to book.
