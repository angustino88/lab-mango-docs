# Lab user guide

As a lab user you can:

- [book a bench](./booking-a-bench.md) for your tests and follow the booking until it is done;
- [manage your bookings](./my-bookings.md): change times, cancel, see approval decisions;
- [follow the lab](./dashboards.md) on the Dashboard, the 3D Dashboard and the Leaderboard;
- [ask the lab team for help](./support-tickets.md) with support tickets.

Pages for spaces, equipment, finance and insights are for coordinators and admins. If you open one, Lab Mango shows
**Access denied (403)**.

## A typical day

1. Open the **Booking Calendar**, find a free slot on the bench you need and create a booking.
2. If it is longer than 4 hours, wait for a coordinator to approve it. **My Bookings** shows the decision.
3. Work at the bench. The booking moves from **Reserved** to **In progress** to **Completed**.
4. If something is broken, create a support ticket and link the bench or the equipment.
