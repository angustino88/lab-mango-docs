# Support desk

Coordinators are **support agents**. You see **all** tickets, not only your own.

## Work a ticket

- **Assign ticket:** assign it to yourself or another agent, or unassign it.
- **Change ticket status:** move it through the workflow. Some transitions ask for a reason or a resolution code.
- **Public reply:** visible to the requester and watchers. Your first public reply stops the response-time clock.
- **Internal note:** visible to agents only. It does not count as a response.
- **Labels, team and parent ticket** can be set by agents only.
- **Link resources:** connect the bench or equipment, so the resource's incident history is complete.

## Response and resolution targets (SLA)

Each priority can have response and resolution targets. A ticket shows **OK**, **At risk**, **Breached**,
**Paused** or **Met**. Statuses that wait for the requester pause the clock. When a target is breached, the
assignee and the team lead are notified.

Workload, SLA health, throughput and satisfaction ratings are available to agents in the ticket statistics.
