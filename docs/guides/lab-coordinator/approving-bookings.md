# Approving bookings

Bookings longer than 4 hours need approval. As a coordinator you see **everyone's** bookings in **My Bookings** and
the calendar, so you can find and decide pending requests.

## Find pending requests

In **My Bookings**, filter the **Approval** column to **Pending**. Only bookings that are **Reserved** and
**Pending** can be decided.

## Approve or reject

- **Approve** (green tick): confirm in **Approve Booking**. The approval becomes **Approved**.
- **Reject** (red cross): give a reason (required, up to 500 characters) in **Reject Booking**. The approval
  becomes **Rejected**.

Lab Mango records who decided and when. A decision is final: deciding again returns a conflict.

:::warning Separation of duties
You cannot approve or reject a booking that you created or that is for you, even as an admin. Ask another
coordinator or admin.
:::

## Change or cancel someone else's booking

Coordinators and admins can edit or cancel any booking that is not completed, expired or cancelled. Use the
**Edit** icon in **My Bookings**, or open the booking in the calendar. Tell the person why.
