# Spaces: rooms, stations and benches

The lab is modelled in three levels: a **room** contains **stations**, and a station holds **benches**. Equipment
is placed on a bench, at a station, in a room, or at an untracked place described by a note.

| Page | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Mapping** | See the whole lab as a diagram: which equipment is connected to which bench and station, and change it |
| **Rooms** | Create and edit rooms: identity, capacity and layout |
| **Stations** | Create and edit stations and the room each belongs to |
| **Benches** | Create and edit benches, their station, configuration and connected equipment |

## Import from Excel

Mapping, Rooms, Stations, the equipment pages and Licenses have an **Excel import and export** button. Use it to
load or update many records at once from a workbook. Check the import result: rows with errors are listed and are
not imported.

## Tips

- Keep names and SKUs consistent; they appear in bookings, dashboards and reports.
- Moving a bench to another station changes where its bookings show on the 3D Dashboard.
