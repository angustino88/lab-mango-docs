# Insights: analytics and monitoring

## Analytics

**Analytics** shows how the lab is used over a chosen time frame: bench occupancy, booked share of working time,
efficiency, uptime and usage by group. Use it to plan capacity and to spot benches that are rarely used.

## Monitoring

**Monitoring** shows the lab computers that run the Lab Mango agent. The agent reports heartbeats, attached and
removed devices, metrics such as disk usage, and alerts. The lab session log shows who logged in and out, and when.
Use it to see whether a bench computer is online, what is connected to it, and who is using it.
